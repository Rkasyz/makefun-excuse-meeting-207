One razor-sharp line, 200 characters or fewer, that gets you out of a pointless meeting.

## Entry

> Sorry — I cannot attend. I am in a pre-meeting about the meeting about the meeting, and we still have not decided whether to meet.

The joke is the recursion: a meeting about the meeting, preceded by a pre-meeting — and after all that process, the group still has not decided whether to meet at all. It is true to office life, needs no explaining, and lands the laugh on the final clause.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` as UTF-8 text with no trailing newline. It self-checks the competition gate (1–200 characters, not only whitespace) and refuses to write an invalid entry. The output is deterministic: the same bytes on every run.
